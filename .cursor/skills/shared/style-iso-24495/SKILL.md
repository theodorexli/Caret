# Required Writing Style

Write in the spirit of ISO 24495 (Plain language): relevant, findable, understandable, and usable. Part 1 sets those principles; Part 2 applies them to legal communication; Part 3 applies them to science writing. Public summary: https://www.iplfederation.org/iso-standard/
If doing so would remove a required technical term or a precise meaning, keep the term. Clarity and accuracy trump style.
